# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Heroes opens to five numbered active formation slots, with the reserve roster in a searchable grid.
- Added role-weighted Battle Score to hero cards and hero details, plus gear swap previews with before and after stats.
- Equipped slots open directly to matching gear, sorted by Biggest upgrade. Inventory uses a portrait picker for recipients and shows transfers, equipment restrictions, and displaced offhands before confirmation.


## 1.0.0

- Added distinct hero signatures, companion instincts, tactical skill combos, and named mastery capstones. Hero and pet ascensions add signature effects at their milestone ranks.
- Added illustrated scenery for all nine biomes beyond Whispering Forest and for all four dungeons. Characters have pixel-art pose frames for idle, travel, attacks, specials, hits, and death.
- Added four dungeon boss patterns with low-health follow-ups, themed boss loot, and mid-wave checkpoint recovery.
- Added choices for the Wandering Merchant, Lost Caravan, and Secret Shrine and three upgrade tracks in Guild Headquarters.
- Added a build journal and stage farming records. Adjusted late-game loot overflow sales, gold, keys, and offline encounter timing.
- Added distinct boss, summon, and hatch audio cues. Existing rosters, parties, equipment, and save profiles load into the release.


## 0.9.1

- New expeditions begin with one summon. Existing parties and cloud saves load normally.
- The war band marches between adventure waves and stages, with smaller sprites, better spacing and feet aligned to the path.
- Gear appears on the battlefield when its source falls. Rarity determines the glow and beam, with gold for Legendary. Gear is collected on victory.
- Inventory now has illustrated Gear, Loot log and Loadouts views. The log identifies each drop and its source when recorded.
- A live battle meter shows current and last wave stats with detailed per-hero breakdowns.

## 0.9.0

- Added the first packaged Everfall build against the 3.0 development handoff.
- Added automatic combat, collection, equipment, dungeons, guild assignments, offline rewards, combat reporting, and local and cloud save paths.
- Included the flat source, automated verification, Firebase rules, and a prebuilt GitHub Pages entry in one phone-friendly archive.
- Documented the remaining asset, ability, balance, performance, and live integration work in BUILD-REPORT.md.
